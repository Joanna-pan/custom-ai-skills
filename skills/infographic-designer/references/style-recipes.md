# Style Recipes

These are starting points, not complete prompts. Combine a recipe with the approved topic, information architecture, composition, format, volume, palette, typography, and output constraints. Keep critical labels outside generated imagery.

## Prompt Builder

For any matrix style, define:

1. Visual language: geometry, line, texture, depth, and material.
2. Typography: role, tone, hierarchy, and legibility.
3. Palette: named roles rather than vague color mood.
4. Information structure: primary composition and reading path.
5. Content density: node count and label length.
6. Image production: for rendered, illustrative, material, or dimensional styles, use `openai/gpt-image-2`, or the best available genuine image-generation model if unavailable.
7. Production boundary: what is generated as imagery and what remains editable.
8. Avoid list: artifacts or clichés that would weaken the chosen direction, including generated words and programmatic pseudo-3D.

## Frequently Used Recipes

### Пластилиновая 3D-графика

Create soft clay-like volumes and tactile 3D icons with a genuine image-generation model, using friendly characters or objects, gentle shadows, a warm modern palette, and a small number of large information modules. Keep typography clean and editable outside the generated imagery. Avoid toy-like clutter, excessive gloss, tiny labels, and SVG-built pseudo-3D.

### Брутализм

Rigid or intentionally disrupted grid, oversized typography, stark contrast, hard-edged blocks, exposed structure, and minimal ornament. Preserve deliberate hierarchy despite the confrontational tone. Avoid random misalignment that harms reading.

### Этнографическая

Use researched local materials, motifs, symbols, craft textures, and a nature-derived palette to connect facts, techniques, and cultural context. Avoid generic "tribal" decoration, cultural mixing, sacred symbols used as ornament, and caricature.

### Рукописный скетч

An intelligent notebook page with hand-drawn headings, arrows, doodle icons, frames, underlines, and marker accents. Use one central idea with clear branches. Keep final text typeset in a handwriting-compatible font when exact spelling matters.

### Анатомическая

Present the subject as an organism or mechanism: outer shell, internal layers, hidden processes, key elements, and relationships. Use a coherent cutaway or object with callouts and roughly 5-7 semantic nodes.

### Лабораторная

Frame the topic as an experiment using specimens, ingredient cards, scales, formulas, observations, and measured outcomes. Distinguish metaphorical laboratory elements from real scientific evidence.

### Редакционный журнал

Use expressive but controlled typography, editorial grid, one strong lead image or diagram, concise text blocks, captions, and generous negative space. The mood is intelligent, calm, and culturally informed.

### Bento Grid

Use 6-8 modules of intentionally varied size with one dominant takeaway, clear grouping, and consistent internal spacing. Cards must reflect information priority rather than arbitrary visual variety.

### Обложка винила

Square album-cover composition with a central image, expressive title, print texture, subtle grain, disc or sleeve cues, label sticker, track-list structures, liner-note details, and 5-7 short content blocks. Avoid making real information illegible for retro authenticity.

### Изометрия

Generate a small coherent 3D world at a consistent isometric angle with a genuine image-generation model. Add editable callouts, arrows, and exact labels during layout. Avoid inconsistent perspective, generated text, programmatic pseudo-3D, and decorative objects without explanatory purpose.

### Музейная экспозиция

Treat the canvas as a contemporary museum panel: authoritative title, restrained explanatory labels, artifact fragments, timeline or catalog numbers, and careful spacing. Use a calm, slightly ceremonial tone.

### Мир в разрезе

Create one continuous cutaway scene revealing internal rooms, layers, actors, processes, and hidden mechanisms. Add 5-7 concise callouts. Keep the scene spatially coherent instead of converting it into unrelated cards.

### Постмодернистский коллаж

Combine book fragments, mirrors, labyrinths, archival cards, footnotes, annotations, and shifts in authorship or reality. Maintain a discoverable information path beneath the conceptual play. Do not fabricate quotations or archival evidence.

### Vaporwave / ретрофутуризм

Use restrained 1980s-1990s retro-futurist cues: luminous grids, sunset colors, chrome or glossy objects, and synthetic display typography. Avoid neon overload and preserve sufficient contrast for explanatory text.

### Dark Academia

Use a dark scholarly palette, paper and book textures, restrained brass or gold accents, marginalia, diagrams, and library references. Avoid low-contrast brown-on-black text and generic mystical symbols unrelated to the topic.

### Временная шкала

Show 5-9 events in chronological order with date, event, and short significance. Use a strong time axis, visible milestones, and consistent interval logic. Signal breaks in scale and uncertain dates explicitly.

### Бумажная аппликация / Paper Cut

Use layered cut-paper shapes, controlled shadows, handmade edges, and simple silhouettes. Keep layers shallow enough that the hierarchy remains clear and labels remain crisp.

### Cartoon

Use simplified expressive characters, clear silhouettes, restrained outlines, and sequential visual actions. Favor explanatory gestures and object relationships over decorative comedy. Keep character design consistent across panels.

### Детская книжная иллюстрация

Use warm narrative scenes, age-appropriate forms, friendly characters, clear emotional cues, and a limited palette. Match vocabulary and density to the stated age group; do not infantilize older audiences.

### Конструктор из деталей

Build the topic from modular interlocking toy-brick-like forms. Use blocks to encode stages, dependencies, or components, maintain consistent scale, and keep labels external. Use a generic construction-toy language unless the user specifically requires branded assets and has the rights to them.

### Главы литературного романа

Organize the infographic as chapters, scenes, motifs, narrators, or turning points. Use metaphorical, layered, moderately surreal imagery while keeping chapter order and interpretive claims clear. Distinguish quotations from paraphrase.

### Финансовая анатомия

Present a budget, business model, cash flow, or investment structure as an annotated system: sources, channels, reserves, costs, risks, and outcomes. Use accurate quantitative charts for measured values and anatomical metaphor only as the organizing illustration.

## Style Combination Rules

- Name one primary style and at most one supporting influence.
- Combine compatible traits, not full aesthetic systems. Example: editorial grid + paper-cut illustration.
- Keep brand, audience, and medium above novelty.
- Translate requests for a living artist into non-identifying traits such as palette, texture, composition, or historical movement.
